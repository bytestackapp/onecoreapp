About OneCore

OneCore is a centralized platform that helps organizations bring people, information, and everyday operations together in one place. It connects administrators, staff, members, students, and parents through a shared platform designed to make communication, coordination, and routine tasks simpler and more organized.

  • Announcements and Notifications
    • Share important updates and keep members informed.
  • Calendar and Events
    • Organize activities, meetings, schedules, and attendance.
  • Groups and Members
    • Manage classes, departments, teams, clubs, and other groups.
  • Attendance Management
    • Record attendance, track work or school-day activity, and submit correction requests.
  • Leave Management
    • Submit, review, and manage leave requests and approvals.
  • Parent and Guardian Access
    • Help authorized parents and guardians stay informed about their linked children's school activities and attendance.
  • Connected Workflows
    • Bring related information and tasks together to reduce disconnected processes and unnecessary manual work.

OneCore supports organization-specific branding and access based on assigned roles, helping each organization manage its own users and information.

OneCore goal is simply make essential organizational information and services easier to access, manage, and connect.

OneCore Support

Need help with OneCore? We're here to help you get back on track.

Admin assistance

Your school or organization's administrator can help with:

  • Account registration and approval
  • Account activation or deactivation
  • Parent-child account linking
  • Group membership and access permissions
  • QR codes and attendance records
  • Organization-specific settings and information

Technical assistance

If you encounter an application error, a page that does not load, or a feature that is not working correctly, try closing and reopening the app and checking your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact the OneCore support team.

support.onecore@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, describe what happened and which page or feature you were on, and mention your device (for example, iPhone or Android). Please never send your password, PIN, or verification codes.

We aim to respond within 2–3 working days. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the issue.

Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

OneCore is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting personal information. This notice explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use OneCore.

OneCore is intended to operate in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Your school or organization generally determines what personal information is collected and how it is used for its operations. Depending on the activity and applicable law, the school or organization may act as the personal information controller, while OneCore and its service providers may process information to provide and maintain the platform.

For questions about records held by your organization, contact its administrator or designated Data Protection Officer, if applicable. For platform-related privacy concerns, contact OneCore support team.

2. What information may be collected?

Depending on the features you use, OneCore may process:

  • Account information, including your name, email address, role, and optional contact details.
  • Organization information, including your membership, groups, classes, departments, and authorized relationships with other users.
  • Attendance information, including attendance status, timestamps, time-in, breaks, time-out, and correction requests.
  • Leave requests, approvals, balances, and related records.
  • Announcements, event details, attendance responses, acknowledgments, and notifications.
  • Technical and security information, such as activity logs, device information, session information, and notification tokens.
  • Location information, if an enabled location feature is used and the required permissions are granted.

Only information relevant to the applicable services and purposes should be processed.

3. How is your information used?

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts and organization memberships.
  • Provide announcements, notifications, calendars, events, and group features.
  • Record and manage attendance and leave requests.
  • Support authorized parent or guardian access to linked children's records.
  • Operate optional features that require location information.
  • Maintain application functionality, investigate errors, and protect accounts and records.
  • Meet applicable legal and organizational obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes that are inconsistent with the purposes communicated to you and applicable law.

4. Who can access your information?

Access depends on the user's role, permissions, and relationship to the relevant organization or records.

Administrators may manage records within their authorized organization. Teachers, group heads, staff, parents, and other members may access information according to their assigned permissions and the features available to them.

Parents and guardians may access records of children properly linked to their accounts, subject to applicable permissions and policies.

OneCore is designed to separate organizations' information. Access must be enforced through the application's authorization controls and backend security policies.

5. Storage and service providers

OneCore uses third-party services to operate the application and provide its features. These may include Vercel for application hosting, Supabase for authentication and data services, and relevant platform providers for push notifications.

These providers may process or store information as needed to deliver their services. Depending on the providers' infrastructure and configurations, information may be processed or stored outside the Philippines.

Third-party services are subject to their own applicable terms, privacy notices, and security practices.

6. Information stored on your device

OneCore may store session information, display preferences, and limited organization-branding information on your device to support sign-in and application functionality.

Scanner devices may also store a limited local roster and QR-code mappings to recognize users when an internet connection is unavailable. Locally queued attendance records may be retained until synchronization is possible.

Protect devices used to access OneCore, particularly shared devices and attendance scanners. Sign out when finished using a shared device, and avoid allowing unauthorized people to access stored information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purposes, applicable legal requirements, and legitimate organizational recordkeeping needs.

Retention periods may differ by record type and organization policy. Authorized administrators may be able to archive, export, or remove certain records, subject to the application's capabilities and applicable requirements.

Deleting information from the application may not automatically remove exported files, local device copies, backups, or records retained where legally necessary. Requests for access, correction, or deletion should be directed to the responsible organization or its designated contact.

8. Optional location features

If an optional location feature is enabled, location information may be collected when the feature is used and the device has granted the required location permissions.

For example, an on-demand child-location feature may request a registered device's location and make it available to an authorized parent or guardian linked to that child. Location access is subject to device permissions, connectivity, operating-system restrictions, and the feature's actual implementation.

Location information should only be accessible to authorized users and retained according to the feature's disclosed retention period. If the device is offline, switched off, or unable to provide a location, a current location may not be available.

9. Children and parent or guardian access

Student accounts and parent-child relationships may be managed by the school or organization. Access to a child's information must be restricted to appropriately authorized users.

Organizations are responsible for determining and fulfilling applicable requirements relating to parental or guardian consent and the processing of children's information. Optional location features require particular care because location data can reveal a child's whereabouts.

10. Security

OneCore uses authentication and role-based access controls intended to protect information from unauthorized access. The platform's security also depends on proper backend permissions, secure device handling, and the practices of the organizations using it.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials, keep their devices secure, and report suspected unauthorized access or security issues promptly.

11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limitations, you may have the right to be informed about processing, access your personal information, request correction, object to or restrict certain processing, request deletion or blocking where applicable, and seek other available remedies.

To exercise these rights, contact your school or organization's administrator or designated Data Protection Officer, if applicable. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through its official website at https://privacy.gov.ph.

12. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when OneCore's features, data practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. Material changes should be communicated as required by applicable law.

13. Contact

For questions about organization records, contact your school or organization's administrator or designated Data Protection Officer.

For platform-related privacy questions, contact support.onecore@gmail.com
