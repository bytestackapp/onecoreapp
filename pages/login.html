About OneCore

OneCore is a centralized platform designed to make organizational operations simpler, more organized, and more connected.

OneCore brings essential information, services, and administrative functions together in one place—helping users access what they need without having to rely on multiple systems or disconnected processes.

Support

Need help with OneCore? If you encounter an issue, have trouble accessing your account, or have questions about a feature, please contact your school or organization's designated administrator or support team.

When reporting an issue, include a brief description of the problem and, if possible, the page or feature where it occurred. Please do not include your password, PIN, verification codes, or other confidential information.

Privacy

OneCore respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. Information collected through the application is used only for legitimate purposes related to providing school services, managing accounts, communication, security, and other authorized functions.

OneCore handles personal information in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Users may have rights to access, correct, or request appropriate handling of their personal information, subject to applicable laws and policies.
