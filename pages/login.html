About OneCore

OneCore is a centralized platform that helps organizations bring people, information, and everyday operations together in one place. It connects administrators, staff, members, students, and parents through a shared platform designed to make communication, coordination, and routine tasks simpler and more organized.

  • Announcements and notifications. Share important updates, ask for acknowledgment, and reach members on their phones.
  • Calendar and events. Organize activities, meetings, and schedules, collect RSVPs, and send reminders.
  • Groups and people. Manage classes, departments, teams, clubs, and other groups, with a directory of the people in your organization.
  • Attendance. Record time in, breaks, and time out with QR scanners, review attendance history, and submit correction requests.
  • Leave management. Submit, review, and track leave requests, balances, and approvals.
  • Parent and guardian access. Help authorized parents and guardians stay informed about their linked children's attendance and school activities.
  • Find My Child (optional). On request, an authorized parent or guardian can see where their child's registered Android phone is right now.
  • Reports and archive. Export attendance records and archive old records each year.
  • Privacy controls. Administrators choose who can see members' contact details.

OneCore supports organization-specific branding and access based on assigned roles, helping each organization manage its own users and information.

The goal of OneCore is simply to make essential organizational information and services easier to access, manage, and connect.

Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

OneCore is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting personal information. This notice explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use OneCore.

OneCore is intended to operate in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Your school or organization generally determines what personal information is collected and how it is used for its operations. Depending on the activity and applicable law, the school or organization may act as the personal information controller, while OneCore and its service providers may process information to provide and maintain the platform.

For questions about records held by your organization, contact its administrator or designated Data Protection Officer, if applicable. For platform-related privacy concerns, contact the OneCore support team.

2. What information may be collected?

Depending on the features you use, OneCore may process:

  • Account information, including your name, email address, role, and an optional phone number.
  • Organization information, including your membership, groups, classes, departments, and authorized relationships with other users, such as a parent linked to a child.
  • Attendance information, including attendance status, scan times (time in, breaks, and time out), and correction requests.
  • Leave requests, approvals, balances, and related records.
  • Announcements, event details, event responses, acknowledgments, and notifications.
  • Activity logs of administrator actions, session information, and the notification address of your device.
  • Information about a student's registered phone for the optional Find My Child feature, such as a random installation ID, app version, and whether location permission is granted.
  • Location information, if the Find My Child feature is used and the required permissions are granted: the location, its accuracy, and the time it was taken, along with a record of the request.
  • Logos and colors that an organization's administrator chooses for its branding. These images are stored publicly so they can appear on the sign-in page.

Only information relevant to the applicable services and purposes should be processed.

3. How is your information used?

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts and organization memberships.
  • Provide announcements, notifications, calendars, events, groups, and the people directory.
  • Record and manage attendance and leave requests.
  • Send parents and guardians notifications about their linked children's attendance.
  • Support authorized parent or guardian access to linked children's records.
  • Operate optional features that require location information.
  • Maintain application functionality, investigate errors, and protect accounts and records.
  • Meet applicable legal and organizational obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes that are inconsistent with the purposes communicated to you and applicable law.

4. Who can access your information?

Access depends on the user's role, permissions, and relationship to the relevant organization or records.

Administrators may manage records within their authorized organization. Teachers, group heads, staff, parents, and other members may access information according to their assigned permissions and the features available to them.

Contact details: your organization's administrator decides who can see members' email addresses and phone numbers in the people directory. The choices are Everyone, Staff Only (students and parents see only the contact details of administrators and teachers), or None (students and parents see no one's contact details). Administrators and teachers can see members' contact details to do their work. Unless your administrator changes it, members can see each other's contact details.

Parents and guardians may access records of children properly linked to their accounts, subject to applicable permissions and policies.

OneCore is designed to keep organizations' information separate from one another. Authorized OneCore platform administrators may access an organization's information when needed to set up, support, or maintain the service.

5. Storage and service providers

OneCore uses third-party services to operate the application and provide its features. These include Vercel for application hosting, Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, and MapTiler, using OpenStreetMap data, to display maps in the Find My Child feature.

These providers may process or store information as needed to deliver their services. Depending on the providers' infrastructure and configurations, information may be processed or stored outside the Philippines. When a map is shown, the map provider can see which map area is being viewed.

Third-party services are subject to their own applicable terms, privacy notices, and security practices.

6. Information stored on your device

OneCore stores session information, display preferences, limited organization-branding information, and a temporary copy of your profile on your device to support sign-in and fast loading. The copy of your profile is refreshed regularly and removed when you sign out.

When you sign out, OneCore also removes your device's notification address from the server, so the device stops receiving your notifications.

Scanner devices store a limited local roster, QR-code mappings, the device's access code, a clock-correction value, and queued attendance scans until they can be sent. Each person's QR code contains a random code, not their name.

Protect devices used to access OneCore, particularly shared devices and attendance scanners. Sign out when finished using a shared device, and avoid allowing unauthorized people to access stored information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purposes, applicable legal requirements, and legitimate organizational recordkeeping needs.

Retention periods differ by record type and organization policy. Administrators can export and archive records for a past year. Archived records are removed from the OneCore database after the export is saved. Exported files are held outside OneCore and are the responsibility of the organization.

Location results from Find My Child are deleted automatically after 24 hours. A record that a request was made, showing who asked about which child but not the location, is kept in the organization's activity history.

Deleting information from the application may not automatically remove exported files, local device copies, backups, or records retained where legally necessary. Requests for access, correction, or deletion should be directed to the responsible organization or its designated contact.

8. Optional location feature (Find My Child)

Find My Child is optional and works with a student's registered Android phone that has OneCore installed and location permission granted. It does not track continuously. When an authorized parent or guardian linked to the child taps Find My Child, the phone is asked for its current location one time and shares it. Each request expires after two minutes.

While the phone is sharing its location, it shows a notice on the screen. The location is visible to the requesting parent or guardian and is deleted after 24 hours. Requests are limited in number, and each request is recorded.

A current location may not be available if the phone is off, offline, has location switched off, or the permission was not granted. Location access is subject to device permissions, connectivity, and operating-system restrictions.

9. Children and parent or guardian access

Student accounts and parent-child relationships are managed by the school or organization. Access to a child's information is restricted to appropriately authorized users.

Organizations are responsible for determining and fulfilling applicable requirements relating to parental or guardian consent and the processing of children's information. Optional location features require particular care because location data can reveal a child's whereabouts.

10. Security

OneCore uses authentication and role-based access controls, enforced by rules in the database, to protect information from unauthorized access. Security also depends on secure device handling and the practices of the organizations using OneCore.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials, keep their devices secure, and report suspected unauthorized access or security issues promptly.

11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limitations, you may have the right to be informed about processing, access your personal information, request correction, object to or restrict certain processing, request deletion or blocking where applicable, and seek other available remedies.

To exercise these rights, contact your school or organization's administrator or designated Data Protection Officer, if applicable. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through its official website at https://privacy.gov.ph.

This notice may be updated when OneCore's features, data practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. Material changes should be communicated as required by applicable law.
